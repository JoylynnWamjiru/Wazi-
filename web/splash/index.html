Public money, in plain language.

Wazi helps you understand how your county government spends public money. Ask a question in Swahili, Sheng, or English on WhatsApp, and get a clear answer drawn straight from official government reports — with the exact source it came from.

For now, Wazi answers questions about Nakuru County. We’re expanding to more counties as we grow — so soon you can ask about yours too.
